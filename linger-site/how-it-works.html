The Linger app · Pilot

A trip planner for your friend group abroad.

Once you're there, the app takes you from "we should go somewhere" to booked, in one shared folder. It's free while we pilot it with students abroad, so expect a few rough edges and tell us what you think. Here's your first trip, start to finish. It takes about five minutes to set up, and your friends need about thirty seconds each.

Getting started

Your first trip

Sign up with your email

No password. Linger emails you a sign-in link. Then add your name and time zone so your crew knows who's who.

Start a trip

Tap New trip and give it a name and a place, like "Prague". It becomes a folder on your Trips page.

Invite your crew

Tap Invite to copy the trip's link and paste it in the group chat. Friends sign up from the link and land right inside the trip.

Fill it together

Everyone adds spots to vote on, pastes TikToks and pins into Inspo, and drops flight and hostel links into Links. Changes show up for everyone live.

Inside a trip

Five tabs, in the order a trip happens.

Plan

Vote, then commit

Add spots and tap to vote; your initials show on every vote. When most of the crew votes for a spot, it joins The list.

  • Reorder the list with arrows
  • Lock the stops that are set
  • Keep hours, cost and a map link on each
Inspo

Saves in one place

Paste an Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest link, or upload a screenshot. Two boards: Places to go and Photo inspo to recreate. Send any place to Plan in one tap.

Links

Every booking, one spot

Flights, trains, stays, tickets and ways to get around, each with its price and a Booked by tag so nobody books twice.

When

Find the dates, send the invite

Add date options. Everyone marks yes, maybe or no. Lock in the one that works, and a shareable invite appears with Going, Maybe and Can't go.

Split

Settle up, simply

Add what you paid and who it was for. Linger shows the fewest payments to make everyone even.

Outside the folders

The rest of your semester.

Trips

Every folder

Grouped into Happening soon, Planning and Traveled. Locking a date moves a trip into Happening soon.

Home

Stay close to your people

Add family and friends back home with their time zones. Linger shows good times to call and text, so you never wake anyone up.

Prep

Get ready

A to-do list, a packing list and a weekend bag list. Check things off, add your own, remove what you don't need.

Profile

You

Your name, where you're studying, your time zone and your home currency.

Questions

Good to know

Does everyone need an account?

Yes, so votes and dates show who's who. It's just an email, and the invite link walks friends through it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open linger in your phone's browser and choose Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app, and trips you've opened recently stay readable without wifi.

Is it free?

Yes. It's a pilot, so it's free while we learn what students actually use.

Who can see my trips?

Only the people in each trip. There are no public profiles and no feed.
