What to pack

Pack once, pack right.

Check things off as you go; your list stays on this device. Add anything that's yours, and skip what you don't need. Check your airline's bag limits before you start.

Leave at home

  • Bedding, towels and kitchen stuff, if your housing provides them
  • Bulk toiletries and school supplies: buy them there
  • Hangers
  • Anything valuable you can't replace

Buy when you get there

  • Hair dryer, straightener or curling iron: US ones can blow a fuse, even with a converter
  • Your go-to cold remedies, if you couldn't bring them (find the pharmacy in week one)
  • A local SIM or eSIM, if your plan doesn't cover it
