Funding 101

How to actually get it

Start with your program

Providers like IES, CIEE and AIFS have their own aid, often with first-gen awards and shorter applications.

Ask your campus office early

Boren and some others need a campus review weeks before the national deadline.

Pell Grant? Look at Gilman

It's built for students receiving Pell, and pairs well with program aid.

Reuse your story

One strong essay on why this place, and what you'll bring back, adapts to most applications.
