No. 01 · The app

Six tabs became one folder.

One weekend trip used to take TikTok saves, a notes doc of places, a scheduling poll, flight tabs, a group chat and a split-bill app. Linger puts the decision in one place, so you spend less time in tabs and more time there.

01

Start a trip

Make a folder — "Prague" — and drop the invite link in the group chat. Friends join without a password.

02

Save & vote

Paste TikToks, Reels and pins. Everyone votes on spots; the ones most of you want make the list.

03

Find the weekend

Each person taps their free weekends. Linger shows where the whole crew lines up.

04

Lock it & go

Send an RSVP invite, track who booked what, and split costs in your home currency.

Why it exists

"A doc full of places, friends with different class schedules, endless flight tabs, and people back home in another time zone."

Linger started in 2021 as Always Through, a small site of resources and scholarships for students getting through hard seasons. After a semester in Copenhagen, it grew into the tool its founder wished she'd had.

    No. 04 · Before you go

    The things nobody told me to think about.

    Small stuff that turned out to matter a lot during my semester in Copenhagen.

    01 · Plugs

    Get the right adapters

    Check the plug type for every country you'll visit, not just where you live. Bring a couple of adapters and a power strip so one adapter charges everything.

    02 · Home

    Set call times early

    Agree on when you're usually free and when your family is, before you leave. A rough weekly rhythm beats guessing across time zones. The call-home clock above helps.

    03 · Money

    Build a cushion

    A side gig before you go, or a few scholarship applications, gives you room to say yes to trips instead of stressing over every euro.

    04 · Keepsakes

    Find your way to remember

    For me it was decorating and curating little photo posts. It kept my family in the loop and kept every memory safe in one place. Find the version that's yours.

    05 · Language

    Learn a little first

    Even a few weeks of Duolingo before you land makes ordering coffee and asking directions feel less scary, and helps a new place feel like yours faster.

    No. 05 · Funding

    Scholarships, so cost doesn't decide for you.

    Study abroad awards first, plus a few for high schoolers. Save the ones you're applying for — they stay on this device. Deadlines last checked Oct 30, 2026; always confirm on the official site before you apply.

    Start with your program

    Providers like IES, CIEE and AIFS have their own aid, often with first-gen awards and shorter applications.

    Ask your campus office early

    Boren and some others need a campus review weeks before the national deadline.

    Pell Grant? Look at Gilman

    It's built for students receiving Pell, and pairs well with program aid.

    Reuse your story

    One strong essay on why this place, and what you'll bring back, adapts to most applications.
