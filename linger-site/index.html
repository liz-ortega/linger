Why it exists

"It's easy to say I'll be okay. Then the time comes, and you'd rather be out enjoying it than scrambling."

Linger started in 2021 as Always Through, a small site of resources and scholarships for students getting through hard seasons. After a semester in Copenhagen, it grew into a guide for going abroad, and a planner for the trips you take while you're there.
